Saved scenarios stay in this browser’s local storage. They are not sent to a server. Reports record model assumptions and prototype limitations alongside the estimates.

Prototype limitation: This is an educational visualization and simplified rigid-wall energy estimate—not a vehicle crash solver, structural durability test, safety rating, certification, or prediction for any production vehicle. It uses no real vehicle CAD, finite-element analysis, injury model, validated material data, or crash-test correlation. Do not use it to design, approve, compare, or certify a vehicle or safety system. Real safety decisions require qualified engineers, validated tools, physical testing, and applicable regulatory procedures.
